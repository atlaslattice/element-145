### Known caveats to preserve
- Two SHUGS operator implementations differ in sigma definition and window weighting; reported numerical scores must not be pooled as identical.
- Hanning endpoints may isolate nodes in the original matrix; do not equate narrative admin-sphere centrality with measured graph connectivity.
- Previously claimed GUE-KS values are not uniformly reproduced by reconstructed numerical runs.
- Full TCSS / Path B matrix entries remain unverified in the cited source reconstruction.
- A spectral matrix's eigenvalues do not calibrate p-B11 cross sections, material resonances, magnetic confinement or reactor performance.
- SHUGS ontology counts (144+1) and HSN address cells (12x12x12=1,728) are architectural representations, not physical periodic-table laws.

### Scientific interface with Stars
SHUGS can consume versioned, provenance-attached scientific data *read-only* to build navigable physical-property maps. It cannot write model parameters, performance multipliers, or calibration status back into Stars. Any proposed future bridging physical hypothesis must be separately approved, unit-consistent, benchmarked against standard methods and externally validated before consideration; Stars physics-only path never waits for it.
